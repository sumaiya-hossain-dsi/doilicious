What is Mishti Doi?

Mishti doi is a traditional Bengali yogurt dessert, slowly baked to create a rich caramel flavour and incredibly smooth, velvety texture. At Doilicious, we keep it simple — real ingredients, no shortcuts, just doi the way it should be.

Our Story

I grew up across eight countries, and through every new place, one thing stayed constant — my mom’s mishti doi. She would recreate it wherever we lived, using simple ingredients she could find, reminding us that home isn’t just a place, but the people, flavours and moments we carry with us.
